Booking protection

1. Both sides agree on scope, price and deadline.

2. Client funds booking through a regulated payment partner (future integration).

3. Creative submits evidence of delivery.

4. Client accepts, or disputes with a reason.

5. Payout or refund follows the agreed rules and dispute decision.

Demo buttons only change local booking status. No funds are collected, held, transferred or refunded.

For production: payment-provider integration, identity checks, payout controls, terms, support, and dispute administration are required.
