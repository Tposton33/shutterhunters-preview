The future of trusted bookings

Meet ShutterSafe.

A booking process designed to help clients and creators agree on the work, document delivery, and resolve issues fairly.

01 · Agree

Set the rate, coverage, deliverables and due date before work begins.

02 · Fund

Use an approved payment provider when real payments become available.

03 · Deliver

Share agreed files or evidence of job completion.

04 · Review

Both parties get a chance to confirm or raise an issue.

05 · Build trust

Verified booking reviews after the real system launches.

Launch status

ShutterSafe is a planned feature, not an escrow service or live payment system. Please don't send money or sensitive information through this preview.
