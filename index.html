SHUTTERSAFE / COMING SOON

Your work. Your agreement. Your peace of mind.

Before a project begins, the client and creator agree on the work in writing. Our planned protected-payment experience is designed so the creator isn't paid out until the agreed delivery is approved — or a dispute is resolved under the contract terms.

01 / CONTRACTPut it in writing.Define the project, deliverables, deadlines, price and revision terms.
02 / BOTH SIGNAgree together.Both the client and creator review and accept the same agreement.
03 / SECURE FUNDSFund the project.Payment would be arranged through a qualified provider, with payout subject to the agreed release terms.
04 / APPROVE & RELEASEDeliver. Confirm. Pay.The creator delivers; the client reviews. Payout follows approval or an agreed dispute-resolution outcome.
Planned feature only — contracts, payment holding, payout release and dispute resolution are not live. No project money is currently accepted or held by ShutterHunters. Funding and release will depend on the payment provider and applicable terms.

ShutterSafe · Coming Soon

One contract.
Confidence on both sides.

The planned ShutterSafe booking flow connects signed project terms, payment-provider funding, delivery approval and a documented path for disputes.

Concept only: Contracts, payment holding, payout release, escrow and dispute handling are not currently available. ShutterHunters does not accept or hold project funds today.
01 · Create the contract

Document the scope, budget, dates, deliverables, revisions and cancellation policy.

02 · Both parties sign

The client and creator each accept the same terms before work begins.

03 · Arrange protected payment

Under the intended model, funds are processed by a qualified payment provider and payout is tied to agreed conditions, rather than released immediately to the creator.

04 · Deliver and confirm

The creator submits the work and the client reviews it. Payout follows approval, an agreed milestone or the applicable dispute-resolution outcome.

05 · Resolve issues fairly

If the parties disagree, they can submit supporting documentation under the contract and provider rules; approval is not assumed.
